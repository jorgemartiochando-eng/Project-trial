Contribution of each factor to the raw gap (log points × 100 ≈ %). Positive = pushes men's pay above women's.

How to read this

  • Raw gap is what you report. Adjusted gap is the difference left after comparing women and men with the same category, family, entity, tenure, working time, performance and age.
  • An adjusted gap that is statistically significant is evidence of unequal pay for equal work and should be addressed before the joint pay assessment.
  • Caution: "explained" is not "justified". If women are under-represented in senior categories, that segregation is itself something Art. 10(2)(c) asks you to analyse.
  • Never control for factors that are themselves gender-biased (e.g. negotiated starting salary, salary history — which Art. 5(2) forbids asking about).

Close unjustified category gaps

For each category above the target gap (and without a justification), the lowest-paid women are raised first to a common floor until the category's mean gap reaches the target. Increases are applied to basic salary.

Applicants must be told the initial pay or its range before interview, and you may not ask about pay history. Suggested advertised range = 25th–75th percentile of current FTE base salary in the category (rounded). Bars show P10–P90 with the suggested range highlighted and the median marked.

Right to information Art. 7

Any worker may request their pay level and the average pay levels by sex for workers doing the same work or work of equal value. You must reply within two months.
